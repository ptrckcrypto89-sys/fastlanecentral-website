Privacy Policy

This policy describes information handling on fastlanecentral.com and the engine build inquiry form.

Inquiry form

The inquiry form runs in your browser and opens a message in your email application. The site does not submit the form to a server or store the fields. The message is sent only if you choose Send in your email application. Your email provider will process the message and its contents.

Information you choose to send

An inquiry may include your name, email address, location, engine specifications, vehicle or project details, and information about the build you are considering. Do not send payment details, passwords, or information you do not want handled through email.

Analytics and technical logs

Optional Google Analytics loads only after you accept analytics cookies in the site consent prompt. Hosting and security services may also process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the site.

Use and retention

Information sent by email is used to review and respond to the project inquiry and to manage any resulting written project scope. Retention depends on the project, business records, and applicable requirements. Requests about information sent to Fast Lane Central can be emailed to contact@fastlanecentral.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@fastlanecentral.com.
