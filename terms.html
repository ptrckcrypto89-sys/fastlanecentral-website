Terms of Service

This website introduces engine build services and lets prospective customers prepare an inquiry by email. It does not take payment, accept an engine, or authorize work.

Project inquiries

Submitting an inquiry or sending an email does not create an accepted order or reserve a build slot. Do not pay, ship an engine, or arrange drop-off until you receive written project instructions.

Written project scope

Before work begins, the project scope and terms will be recorded in a written quote or work order. It should identify the engine and known condition, agreed work, parts responsibility, any outside machining or specialist work, schedule, payment terms, logistics, and any testing or performance commitments. A project begins only after the written scope is accepted.

Build information

Examples on this site describe possible project paths. They are not a promise of a particular horsepower, reliability, service life, emissions result, completion date, or fitment. Engine condition, parts, fuel, tuning, installation, and use affect results. Any specific commitment must appear in the accepted written scope.

Customer-provided information and parts

Provide accurate engine specifications, history, measurements, and parts information. The written project scope will identify what parts are supplied by the customer and how changes or newly discovered conditions are handled.

Contact

Questions about a proposed engine project can be sent to contact@fastlanecentral.com. The accepted written project scope controls project-specific terms, subject to applicable law.
